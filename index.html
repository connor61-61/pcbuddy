Windows: Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager, then click the Performance tab. Click CPU on the left to see your processor name in the top-right corner, and click GPU to see your graphics card name. The Memory tab shows your installed RAM.

For a fuller readout, press Win + R, type dxdiag, and hit Enter — the System tab lists your CPU and RAM, and the Display tab lists your GPU.

You can also check Settings → System → About for your CPU and RAM at a glance, or open Device Manager and expand Display adapters for your GPU.

Mac: Click the Apple menu → About This Mac to see your chip and memory. Most Macs use integrated graphics, so there's usually no separate GPU to enter.
