This index is simulated in your browser (a random walk seeded on load) so the graph has something to move — it is not pulled from a real market. Live retail prices can't be fetched reliably from client-side JS alone due to CORS restrictions on most retailer and API sites; wiring this to real numbers means pointing it at a small backend or a pricing API (e.g. PCPartPicker's data, a retailer API, or your own scraper) that fetches on a schedule and serves the results here.

PCBuddy gives estimates based on typical performance and pricing data, not live benchmarks — treat results as a general guide.
